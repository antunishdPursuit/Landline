# Breakfast

Breakfast is served in the main restaurant on the ground floor from 6:30 AM
to 10:30 AM daily (weekdays and weekends), Pacific Time. Room service breakfast
can be ordered until 10:00 AM using the in-room dining menu.

A continental breakfast (pastries, fruit, coffee, tea, juice) is available in
the lobby lounge from 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM for guests who prefer a lighter,
faster option.

Breakfast is included for guests booked on the Bed & Breakfast rate plan.
All other guests may purchase breakfast at the restaurant for $28 per person,
plus applicable tax and gratuity.
