# General Policies

**Cancellation**: Reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours
before the scheduled arrival date. Cancellations within 48 hours are charged
one night's room rate plus tax.

**Smoking**: This is a 100% smoke-free property, including guest rooms and
balconies. A cleaning fee of $250 applies if smoking is detected in a room.

**Minimum Age**: Guests checking in must be at least 21 years of age and
present a valid government-issued photo ID and credit card at check-in.

**Quiet Hours**: Quiet hours are observed from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Noise
complaints during this window may result in a courtesy call from the front
desk or, for repeat issues, a request to vacate the property without refund.
