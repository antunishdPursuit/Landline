# Wi-Fi

Complimentary standard Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel, including
guest rooms, the lobby, the restaurant, and the fitness center. The network
name is "HotelGuest" and no password is required — guests accept the terms
of service on the landing page after connecting.

Premium high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 100 Mbps, suitable for video calls and
streaming) is available for $12.99 per day, or complimentary for guests
enrolled in the loyalty program at Gold tier or above.

For connection issues, guests can call the front desk at extension 0 or
visit the business center for wired internet access.
