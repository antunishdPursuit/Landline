# Amenities

**Pool & Hot Tub**: Outdoor pool and hot tub are open daily from 7:00 AM to
10:00 PM. Towels are provided poolside. Children must be accompanied by an
adult at all times.

**Fitness Center**: Open 24 hours daily, accessible with a room key. Includes
cardio machines, free weights, and a stretching area.

**Spa**: Open 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, appointments recommended and can be booked
through the concierge or extension 4.

**Parking**: Self-parking is $35 per night; valet parking is $48 per night.
Both include in-and-out privileges for the duration of the stay.

**Pet Policy**: Dogs and cats under 50 lbs are welcome for a $75 non-refundable
cleaning fee per stay. Maximum two pets per room.
