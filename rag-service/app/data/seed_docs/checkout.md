# Check-in and Check-out

Standard check-in time is 3:00 PM Pacific Time. Standard check-out time is
11:00 AM Pacific Time.

Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to room
availability, and may incur an additional fee:
- Late check-out until 2:00 PM: complimentary for loyalty program members,
  $25 for all other guests.
- Late check-out after 2:00 PM: charged at half the nightly room rate.

Luggage storage is available at the front desk free of charge before check-in
and after check-out on the day of arrival or departure.
