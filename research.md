# What type of landlines hotels use
 
## 1. In-room handsets
 
- **Analog phones**: connects through old copper wiring
- **IP/VoIP phones**: connects through the hotel's network/internet cable
- **Hybrid**: run both at once; analog in guest rooms, IP for front desk/back office
## 2. The switchboard (PBX)
 
- Can be a physical box installed in the hotel, or hosted in the cloud by an outside provider
- **What it does**: Acts like a traffic controller for phone calls inside the hotel. When someone dials an extension (like room service or front desk), the PBX figures out where that call needs to go and connects it.
## 3. The trunk (outside line)
 
- Connection between the hotel and the outside phone network
- Old way = physical phone lines
- New way = calls sent over the internet
- This is the layer our AI system needs to plug into
---
 
# Who still uses traditional phone lines (landlines/VoIP)?
 
- Luxury and resorts
- Large business & convention hotels
- Historic hotels
---
 
# What are modern hotels using?
 
- **In-room tablets**
- **Guest apps**: turns guest's own smartphone into room key, TV remote, front-desk hotline
- **SMS & WhatsApp**: text-only communication directly with front desk (common in budget or boutique hotels)
- **QR codes**: digital menus and service requests
---
 
# How does CRM work?
 
**CRM for hotels**: software that stores guest info, preferences, and stay history so the hotel can personalize service and marketing over time.
